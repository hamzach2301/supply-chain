Method note

The service trains a real Ridge regression model when it starts. It first makes an 80/20 split using shipment identifiers, then learns numeric scaling and category levels only from the training records. Test metrics are computed exclusively from the held-out records. Scenario outputs show model associations based on the synthetic data-generating process; they do not prove causal effects.
